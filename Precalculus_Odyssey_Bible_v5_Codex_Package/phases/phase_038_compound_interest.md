## Prerequisite Review
- Convert percent rates to decimals before using a formula.
- Periodic compounding uses A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt).
- Continuous compounding uses A = Pe^(rt).
- The principal P is the starting amount.
- The exponent nt counts the total number of compounding periods.

## Core Concepts
- More frequent compounding changes both the periodic rate and the number of periods.
- The annual rate r is divided by n before it is applied each period.
- The exponent nt multiplies years by periods per year.
- Interest earned is final amount minus principal.
- Continuous compounding is the limiting case A = Pe^(rt).
- Solving for principal means dividing the future amount by the growth factor.

## Common Misconceptions
- Using r = 5 instead of r = 0.05 for 5 percent.
- Putting n only in the exponent and not in the periodic rate.
- Using simple interest when the problem says compounded.
- Forgetting that monthly compounding has 12 periods per year.
- Rounding inside the exponent before evaluating the final amount.

## Core Concept
- More frequent compounding changes both the periodic rate and the number of periods.
- The annual rate r is divided by n before it is applied each period.
- The exponent nt multiplies years by periods per year.
- Interest earned is final amount minus principal.
- Continuous compounding is the limiting case A = Pe^(rt).
- Solving for principal means dividing the future amount by the growth factor.

## Worked Example
- Invest 1000 dollars at 6 percent compounded annually for 3 years. What is the amount? Answer: $1191.02 Reason: Use A = 1000(1.06)^3. Since 1.06^3 = 1.191016, the amount is $1191.02.
- In A = 2500(1 + 0.04/4)^(4t), what are P, r, and n? Answer: P = 2500, r = 0.04, n = 4 Reason: The form is P(1 + r/n)^(nt). Here the principal is 2500, the annual rate is 0.04, and quarterly compounding means n = 4.
- Find the amount from 2000 dollars at 8 percent compounded quarterly for 1 year. Answer: $2164.86 Reason: Use A = 2000(1 + 0.08/4)^4 = 2000(1.02)^4. This equals about $2164.86.
- For 500 dollars at 12 percent compounded monthly for 2 years, what expression gives the amount? Answer: 500(1.01)^24 Reason: The monthly rate is 0.12/12 = 0.01 and the number of months is 12(2) = 24.

## Common Mistakes
- Using r = 5 instead of r = 0.05 for 5 percent.
- Putting n only in the exponent and not in the periodic rate.
- Using simple interest when the problem says compounded.
- Forgetting that monthly compounding has 12 periods per year.
- Rounding inside the exponent before evaluating the final amount.
