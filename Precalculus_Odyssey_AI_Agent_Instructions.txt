PRECALCULUS ODYSSEY — MASTER INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI AGENTS
=======================================================

The project has accumulated overlapping plans, partially completed systems, and older requirements that are no longer fully valid. Before writing more code, treat this document as the latest source of truth.

PRIORITY OF INSTRUCTIONS
------------------------

When requirements conflict, follow this order:

1. The user's latest direct instructions
2. handoff.md for the current architecture and completed systems
3. knowledge.md for environment, testing, backup, and workflow rules
4. ROOMS_AND_CODES.md for the current room structure and Worm Hole codes
5. Older design documents only when they do not conflict with the above

The user's latest requirements override older plans involving Monster Essence, Star Village, old navigation, and incomplete economy systems.

The project is already modularized and must continue using ordered classic scripts so it works through both file:// and localhost. Do not convert it to ES modules. Do not add a build system unless the user explicitly requests one.

REQUIRED FEATURE DECISIONS
--------------------------

1. ALL ENEMIES ARE AI ROBOTS

The word "monster" may remain as casual player-facing language, but technically every enemy is an AI robot, guardian, drone, evolved machine, or corrupted Archive construct.

Enemy drops should become:

- AI Chip
- CPU
- GPU
- Neural Processor
- Quantum Chip
- Alien Processor
- Robotic Alloy
- Energy Core

These components should upgrade:

- Weapons
- Shields
- Armor
- Shoes
- Spell devices
- Elemental equipment

Remove Monster Essence from new gameplay. If older saves contain Essence, migrate it into an appropriate chip or component.

2. STAR ATLAS MUST BE GLOBAL

The Star Atlas button must remain available from almost every major screen.

The first Star Atlas page should show star systems, not every planet at once.

Each star-system card should show:

- Name
- Current-player-location marker
- Locked or unlocked status
- Number of planets
- Short interesting fact
- Travel availability

Clicking a star system should open its planet list.

Reuse and extend the existing Star Log, astronomy data, stylized planets, Sol-system room theming, and Archive Minds story instead of rebuilding them from nothing.

3. PLANET PAGES MUST REMAIN QUESTION-FOCUSED

The planet page should devote most of its area to:

- Math question
- Equation tiles
- Operation controls
- Progress
- Essential battle or mission controls

Planet facts must live behind a compact Planet Info button, modal, drawer, or side panel.

Do not surround the equation with every RPG system.

4. EARTH REPLACES STAR VILLAGE

Earth becomes the permanent home hub.

Add a global "Go Back to Earth" button whenever the player is away from Earth.

During combat, clicking it should open a confirmation such as:

"Return to Earth and retreat from this battle? The current enemy's health will reset."

Earth should contain all facilities previously associated with Star Village.

5. PROFILE AND INVENTORY

The player profile should remain visible globally.

Double-clicking the profile must open a large dedicated inventory and equipment interface, not a tiny tooltip.

Also provide a normal single-click or button alternative for touch devices and accessibility.

The profile interface must show:

Character information:
- Player name
- Level
- Experience
- Health
- Mana
- Attack
- Defense
- Speed
- Elemental affinities

Currencies:
- Cash
- Gold
- Silver
- Gems

Equipped items:
- Weapon
- Shield
- Armor
- Shoes
- Accessories
- Installed AI chips

Inventory:
- Weapons
- Shields
- Shoes
- Armor
- Consumables
- AI chips
- Materials
- Quest items
- Spells
- Elemental items

Actions:
- Equip
- Unequip
- Inspect
- Compare
- Upgrade
- Sell

6. WONDERLAND AND HOO HEY HOW

Wonderland must exist on Earth.

Hoo Hey How must appear inside Wonderland and have an obvious entrance.

Do not leave this only in a design document.

7. TRADING ROOM

The Trading Room must support exchange among:

- Cash
- Gold
- Silver
- Gems

It must include:

- Buy price
- Sell price
- Current change
- Historical chart
- Holdings
- Transaction history
- Changing prices

Preserve the prior rule that prices change only after completing a review question, unless the user later changes that rule.

The review-question pool should include all math learned up to the player's current room.

8. LARGE WEAPON CATALOGUE

Create a large data-driven weapon catalogue, not five text entries.

Each weapon needs:

- Illustrated SVG or image
- Name
- Category
- Rarity
- Attack
- Speed
- Accuracy
- Critical chance
- Wu Xing element
- Special effect
- Upgrade level
- AI-chip slots
- Price
- Description

Suggested categories:

- Swords
- Daggers
- Spears
- Bows
- Staffs
- Scepters
- Hammers
- Axes
- Energy blades
- Laser weapons
- Plasma weapons
- Quantum weapons
- Ancient AI weapons

Do not use emoji as final weapon art.

Use reusable deterministic SVG generators where possible instead of manually drawing hundreds of unrelated assets.

9. WU XING SYSTEM

Implement:

- Wood
- Fire
- Earth
- Metal
- Water

Generating cycle:

Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood

Overcoming cycle:

Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood

Do not reduce Wu Xing to a simple rock-paper-scissors damage multiplier.

Support:

- Weapon element
- Shield element
- Armor resistance
- Planet affinity
- Enemy affinity
- Spell affinity
- Chip affinity
- Generating bonuses
- Overcoming bonuses
- Weaknesses
- Resistance
- Elemental status effects

10. SPELL SYSTEM

Required spell categories:

- Freeze
- Poison
- Burn
- Armor break
- Shield
- Healing
- Speed boost
- Accuracy reduction
- AI hacking
- Chip disruption
- Wood attack
- Fire attack
- Earth attack
- Metal attack
- Water attack

Each spell should define fields similar to:

{
  id,
  name,
  icon,
  element,
  manaCost,
  targetType,
  power,
  duration,
  cooldown,
  statusEffect,
  description
}

Freeze and poison must be real turn-based status effects, not cosmetic animations.

IMPLEMENTATION RULES

3. Keep the main math screen uncluttered.

7. Update handoff.md during every code-changing turn.
